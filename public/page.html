Method

Provider chance: the win, draw and loss probabilities published for each fixture.

My form rating: points per game for each side from the table, a home edge, and a flat 26% draw share, turned into a three-way chance.

Champions League: the provider chance is DraftKings' moneyline for each fixture with the bookmaker margin removed, and my form rating is a goals model built from each club's games this season in all competitions, with a home edge.

Final chance: 50% provider plus 50% my form rating. The pick is the most likely of home, draw or away. High is 60% or more, Medium is 48% or more, otherwise Low (close to a coin toss).

Limits: no goals data yet, so there are no over/under goals, both-teams-to-score, lineups, injuries or player props for football. Form ratings use only this season's points per game, and the sample is under ten games per team. This is not betting advice.

Method

Model score: each side's points per game blended with what the other side allows (home and away splits included), plus a small home edge.

Final spread and total: 35% model, 65% bookmaker line. Win chance: 40% model, 60% moneyline price with the bookmaker margin removed.

Confidence: High needs about 68% or more with the model and market agreeing on the winner. Medium needs 58% or more. A total lean needs the model to be 3 or more points off the posted total.

Limits: seven weeks of games, injuries and lineups are in each game's breakdown but are not yet worked into the model score, and weather is not in, and the lines are the best prices across several books so numbers can differ slightly by book. This is not betting advice.
